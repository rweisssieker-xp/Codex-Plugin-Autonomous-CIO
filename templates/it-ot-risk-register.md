## Escalation Rules

- Escalate to CIO when production continuity or board/customer commitment is affected.
- Escalate to CISO when access, vulnerability or cyber exposure affects OT or regulated systems.
- Escalate to Quality/Regulatory when evidence, validated change or audit traceability is incomplete.
- Escalate to Finance when mitigation requires funding, vendor change or material delay.
