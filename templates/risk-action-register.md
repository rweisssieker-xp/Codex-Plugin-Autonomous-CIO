## Escalation Rules

- Escalate immediately when impact is critical and urgency is now.
- Escalate when owner or decision authority is unclear.
- Escalate when the same risk blocks multiple teams, systems or programs.

## Follow-up Cadence

- Red: daily until controlled.
- Amber: weekly until trend improves.
- Green: monthly or normal governance cadence.
