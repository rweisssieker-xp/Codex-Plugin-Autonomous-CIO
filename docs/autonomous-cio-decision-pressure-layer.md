# Autonomous CIO Decision Pressure Layer

This layer turns pressure around transformation, vendors, compliance, board approval, operating risk and roadmap priorities into explicit decision artifacts.

## Added USPs

- Transformation Kill Criteria: defines stop, change and continue triggers for initiatives with weak value, evidence, architecture or control posture.
- Vendor Negotiation Brief: prepares negotiation asks, fallback position and legal/commercial review needs.
- Compliance Evidence Pack: drafts evidence pack structure with control objective, owner, evidence, decision log, residual risk and follow-up.
- Board Decision Simulator: simulates likely board reactions to approve, defer, approve with conditions or escalate options.
- Operating Risk Heatmap: creates Green/Amber/Red heatmap across delivery, finance, security/control, customer and capacity domains.
- Autonomous Roadmap Reprioritizer: recommends what to promote, protect, pause or defer based on risk, value, evidence and scarce capacity.

## Boundary

The layer prepares decision criteria and drafts. It does not stop projects, negotiate contracts, certify compliance, approve board decisions or change roadmaps externally.
