# Autonomous CIO Innovation Layer 2

This layer expands The Autonomous CIO from decision readiness into operating-system control. It adds deadline pressure, vendor optionality, regulatory shock readiness, cyber business translation, talent bottleneck detection, capital allocation support, post-decision learning and maturity scoring.

## Added USPs

- Decision SLA Enforcer: assigns 24h, 7d or 30d decision windows from board risk, decision debt and missing evidence.
- Vendor Exit Simulator: frames renegotiate, narrow scope, dual-source, insource, exit or monitor options.
- Regulatory Shock Simulator: prepares the minimum response pack for audit, regulator, customer or compliance scrutiny.
- Cyber Business Impact Translator: converts security and control signals into continuity, trust, customer, audit and risk-acceptance decisions.
- Talent Criticality Radar: detects scarce-role, owner-capacity, overloaded-team and key-person risks.
- Capital Allocation Copilot: turns spend, budget, forecast and value signals into funding trade-offs.
- Post Decision Learning Loop: captures which assumptions became true, which risks changed and what memory must update.
- CIO OS Maturity Index: scores whether the organization is operating as ad hoc CIO work, decision toolkit, governed copilot or CIO operating system.

## Safety Boundary

All additions are decision-support artifacts. They do not inspect live contracts, systems, HR data, security tools, financial ledgers or regulatory sources unless the user provides context.
