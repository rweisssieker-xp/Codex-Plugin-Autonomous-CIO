# Autonomous CIO Trust and Resilience Layer

This layer expands The Autonomous CIO with trust, resilience, due diligence, AI portfolio governance, cost-of-delay and commitment tracking. It helps leadership prepare for questions and consequences before risk becomes visible externally.

## Added USPs

- Autonomous Due Diligence Questions: generates targeted questions with evidence needs for board, vendor, audit, AI, transformation and crisis reviews.
- Resilience Continuity Planner: drafts minimum continuity plans and first 24h actions for outage, recovery, integration and customer-impact signals.
- Customer Trust Impact Radar: translates service, billing, outage, communication and recovery signals into customer trust exposure.
- AI Portfolio Governance: turns AI experiments into governed portfolio items with business owner, data owner, risk tier, evaluation baseline and rollback path.
- Cost of Delay Calculator: estimates qualitative cost of delay from value-at-risk, board risk, decision debt, audit exposure, customer trust and scarce capacity.
- Executive Commitment Tracker: tracks draft and prior executive commitments, owners, status and next checks.

## Boundary

These features prepare executive judgment. They do not run incident command, approve AI use cases, send communications, persist commitments or calculate audited financial exposure.
