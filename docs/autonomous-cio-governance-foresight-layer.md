# Autonomous CIO Governance Foresight Layer

This layer adds governance foresight for audit, platform rationalization, data sovereignty, operating-model debt, strategic options and decision war rooms.

## Added USPs

- Audit Finding Predictor: predicts likely audit findings from missing evidence, weak controls and unclear ownership.
- Platform Rationalization Advisor: recommends consolidation, retirement, renegotiation or stabilization from platform and vendor signals.
- Data Sovereignty Radar: detects privacy, residency, retention and cross-border transfer readiness gaps.
- Operating Model Debt Ledger: captures unclear ownership, overloaded governance and decision bottleneck debt.
- Strategic Option Portfolio: frames options by value posture, risk posture and next evidence.
- Executive Decision War Room: prepares roles, first 60 minutes and exit criteria for high-pressure decisions.

## Autopilot Review Additions

- `audit_finding_predictor`
- `platform_rationalization_advisor`
- `data_sovereignty_radar`
- `operating_model_debt_ledger`
- `strategic_option_portfolio`
- `executive_decision_war_room`

## Boundary

These features provide foresight and decision preparation. They do not certify audits, retire platforms, approve data transfers, change operating models or start war rooms automatically.
