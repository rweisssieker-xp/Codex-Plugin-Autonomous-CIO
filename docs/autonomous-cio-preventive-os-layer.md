# Autonomous CIO Preventive OS Layer

This layer expands The Autonomous CIO toward a preventive enterprise operating system. It helps leadership see decision backlog, control-tower status, carve-out readiness, data trust, architecture runway and executive narrative before risk becomes escalation.

## Added USPs

- Executive Decision Backlog: turns scattered decision debt into prioritized backlog items with routing and required input.
- Enterprise Control Tower: summarizes decision readiness, board risk, evidence confidence, truth gaps and decision debt as operating panels.
- M&A Carve-out Readiness: prepares integration, separation and carve-out readiness questions across systems, data, vendors, access, finance, customers and controls.
- Data Trust Radar: checks decision data for owner, definition, lineage, freshness and privacy classification.
- Architecture Runway Guardian: protects scarce architecture capacity, integration readiness and technical-debt guardrails.
- Executive Narrative Generator: creates concise board, CEO, CFO, CISO/Audit and customer narrative lines without hiding uncertainty.

## Boundary

The layer prepares operating intelligence. It does not claim live telemetry, transaction advice, legal review, data certification or architecture approval.
