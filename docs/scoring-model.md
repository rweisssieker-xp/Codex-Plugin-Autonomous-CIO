# Scoring Model

The Autonomous CIO uses a consistent executive scoring language across skills. Use directional scores when exact data is missing.

## Status

- `Green`: controlled, no executive intervention needed.
- `Amber`: material risk or uncertainty, management attention needed.
- `Red`: high impact, urgent decision, escalation or corrective action needed.
- `Unknown`: evidence is insufficient; identify missing data.

## Impact

- `5 Critical`: enterprise-level business, regulatory, security, customer or financial impact.
- `4 High`: major function, strategic program, critical platform or executive commitment affected.
- `3 Medium`: meaningful team, process, portfolio or budget impact.
- `2 Low`: localized impact with limited dependencies.
- `1 Minimal`: informational or low operational consequence.

## Urgency

- `5 Now`: action needed within 24 hours.
- `4 This week`: action needed within 7 days.
- `3 This month`: action needed within 30 days.
- `2 This quarter`: action needed this quarter.
- `1 Monitor`: watch, no immediate action.

## Confidence

- `High`: multiple sources or strong direct evidence.
- `Medium`: plausible evidence but some gaps.
- `Low`: weak evidence, inferred pattern or user-provided partial context.

## Decision Quality

Score proposals by:

- evidence strength
- option clarity
- dependency visibility
- risk ownership
- reversibility
- value traceability
- implementation readiness

## Prioritization Formula

Use this qualitative order:

1. Red status with high impact and high urgency.
2. Decisions blocking multiple teams, systems or programs.
3. Risks with cascading dependencies.
4. Value leakage with near-term recoverability.
5. Strategic drift that undermines stated priorities.

## Output Rule

Every severe recommendation should include:

- why now
- what happens if ignored
- recommended owner
- first action
- evidence confidence
