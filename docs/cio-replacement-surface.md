# CIO Replacement Surface

This document defines where The Autonomous CIO can replace preparation work and where human accountability remains mandatory.

## Automated or Deterministic

- Decision readiness scoring
- Evidence graph generation
- Risk-chain mapping
- Privacy indicator scanning
- Operating rhythm generation
- Score and trend comparisons

## Drafted by Plugin

- Board-ready briefings
- Executive Decision Packets
- Board challenge questions
- Action ledgers
- Approval and escalation drafts
- Decision memory updates

## Decision-Supported

- Approve, defer or approve with conditions
- Risk acceptance
- Budget and capacity trade-offs
- Vendor or architecture decisions
- AI governance approval

## Human-Only

- Final accountability
- External execution
- Regulatory, legal, HR or financial reporting decisions
- Security incident sign-off
- Irreversible actions

## Product Claim

The Autonomous CIO can make CIO work mostly decision-ready before the CIO enters the room. It must not claim to replace accountable leadership.
