# Autonomous CIO Decision Governance Layer

This layer strengthens The Autonomous CIO as a governed operating system by clarifying decision rights, strategy fit, risk acceptance, service health, knowledge continuity and dependency breakpoints.

## Added USPs

- Decision Rights Mapper: maps approver, accountable owner, contributor and approval mode.
- OKR Strategy Fit Checker: checks whether work advances objectives, key results, value or customer outcomes.
- Risk Acceptance Docket: drafts risk acceptance items with owner, expiry, compensating controls and evidence.
- Service Health Sentinel: translates incident, SLA, outage, ticket and recovery signals into executive health posture.
- Knowledge Continuity Planner: reduces key-person and critical-knowledge risk.
- Dependency Breakpoint Analyzer: identifies where owners, systems, integrations, controls or capacity can break execution.

## Boundary

The layer recommends governance structure and operating controls. It does not assign real authority, accept risk, monitor live services or certify strategy alignment without provided evidence.
