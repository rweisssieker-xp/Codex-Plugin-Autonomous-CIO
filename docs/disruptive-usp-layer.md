# Disruptive USP Layer

The Autonomous CIO includes an explicit disruption layer for governed CIO work replacement. The goal is not uncontrolled execution; the goal is to make 70-90% of recurring CIO preparation work decision-ready before leadership enters the room.

## New Marketplace Differentiators

- CIO Work Autonomy Map: decomposes CIO work into automated, drafted, decision-supported and human-only tasks.
- Board Objection Simulator: generates likely CEO, CFO, CISO, Audit, regulator, customer and Board objections with weak-answer risk.
- Decision Debt Ledger: converts delayed, implicit and ownerless decisions into clearance actions.
- Truth Gap Detector: finds gaps between reporting narratives, facts, assumptions, contradictions and missing evidence.
- Executive Time Saved Estimator: estimates prepared executive work and the roles benefiting from that preparation.
- CIO Shadow Agenda: reveals the real CIO agenda based on attention pressure, truth gaps, decision debt and value risk.
- Autonomous Steering Pack Factory: turns mixed context into a steering or board pack outline with decision requests and safeguards.
- Risk Chain Forecast: forecasts the next likely escalation if owners, evidence or decisions remain unresolved.
- Strategic Drift Detector: detects initiatives drifting from strategy, value, architecture, controls or adoption.
- Human Control Contract: defines what AI may observe, advise, draft or prepare, and what remains human-only.

- `human_control_contract`

## Marketplace Safety

These USPs remain connector-neutral in version 0.1. They use only provided context, mark missing evidence, avoid live-access claims and do not execute external actions.
